Brutal Lasagna

Get ready for the most brutal lasagna of your entire life!

This lasagna was made to feed the gods themselves! It is filled with rich and nourishing ingredients that will prep you for the battles ahead!

Ride with us on this delicious, meaty and cheesy adventure towards a meal you'd give up an eye for!

Ingredients

Cheese Filling

  • 15 oz. Skyr cheese, 2 cups
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 cups Jarlsberg cheese
  • 3/4 cup Norvegia cheese, freshly grated
  • 2 teaspoons Nordic seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 tsp pepper

Meat Sauce

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, finely diced
  • 3/4 lb. ground beef
  • 3/4 lb. ground reindeer sausage
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely diced
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 40 oz. marinara sauce
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1 tablespoon hot sauce
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce

Lasagna Noodles/Cheese Topping

  • 12 lasagna noodles, plus extra in case of breakage
  • 2 1/2 cups Jarlsberg cheese

Equipment

  • 9 x 13-inch casserole dish
  • Large pot
  • Large sauce pan

Instructions

Prep Work

  1. Combine the cheese filling ingredients in a medium bowl and set aside. Measure out the remaining ingredients.

Make the Meat Sauce

  1. Heat olive oil over medium heat in a large sauce pan and add the diced onions Let them soften for 10 minutes. (This allows them to release a little sugar which is a nice offset to the acidity of the tomatoe.)
  2. Add the ground beef and sausage and increase heat to medium-high. Use a spatula to break up the meat so that it's very fine and crumbled. Cook for 8-10 minutes, or until cooked through. Add garlic during the last minute. Drain excess grease.
  3. Add the chicken broth and use the silicone spatula to "clean" the bottom and sides of the pot. Add the marinara sauce, tomato paste, hot sauce, and Worcestershire sauce. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cover partially and let the sauce simmer gently while you boil the pasta noodles.

Preheat Oven and Cook the Lasagna Noodles

  1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees.
  2. Begin boiling a large pot of salted water for the lasagna noodles. Once a rapid boil is reached, cook the noodles to al dente according to package instructions. (Set a timer to ensure you don't overcook them.) Gently stir with a wooden spoon throughout cooking to prevent the noodles from sticking. Drain and rinse with cold water until noodles are completely cool.
  3. Lay the cooled noodles flat on wax or parchment paper while you begin assembling the lasagna

Assemble

  1. Spread 1 heading cup of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9 x 13-inch casserole dish, Next, add 4 lasagna noodles, overlapping if needed.
  2. Spread 1/3 of the cheese mixture over the noodles, followed by about 1 1/2 cups of meat sauce.
  3. Top with 4 more lasagna noodles, 1/3 of the cheese mixture, 1 1/2 cups meat sauce.
  4. Finish with 4 more lasagna noodles, the rest of the cheese mixture, and the rest of the meat sauce.
  5. Top with 2 1/2 cups of Jarlsberg cheese.
  6. Spray foil with nonstick cooking sprayand place it spray-side-down on top of the baking dish to prevent the cheese from stickng to it.

Bake

  1. Bake for 35 minutes. Remove cover and bake for 10 more minutes.
  2. Remove and garnish with fresh parsley. Let it sit for 15 minutes prior to serving to allow the layers to set. Serve with garlic bread with cheese.
